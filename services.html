Questions?

Frequently Asked

How far in advance should we book?

For weddings and large events, we recommend booking 6 to 12 months in advance. For studio portraits and commercial shoots, 3 to 4 weeks is usually sufficient.

Do you travel for destination shoots?

Absolutely! We love traveling for destination weddings and remote commercial projects. Custom travel fees are calculated based on location.

When will we receive our photos?

Portrait and commercial galleries are delivered within 2 weeks. Full wedding and massive event galleries typically take 4 to 6 weeks for meticulous editing.

Do we get the raw/unedited files?

We do not provide RAW files. A significant part of our artistic style is the professional editing and color grading applied to the final curated images.
